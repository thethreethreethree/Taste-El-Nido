Our Story

Born from the
heart of Palawan

Nestled on Rizal Street in the heart of El Nido, taste. is more than a café — it's a gathering place for travelers and locals alike. We blend modern café culture with the warmth of island living, serving globally-inspired food made with the freshest local ingredients.

From our locally roasted espresso to our vibrant smoothie bowls, every dish is crafted with intention. We believe great food should be beautiful, honest, and kind to our planet.

The Experience

More than a meal —

Two floors of art, flavour, and community. Our café is designed to slow you down, help you breathe, and remind you that you're in one of the most beautiful places on earth.

  • 01

    Community-Centered Space
