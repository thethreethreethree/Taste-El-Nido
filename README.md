# Taste El Nido
Taste El Nido
aste El Nido
Taste El Nido is a locally owned café and eatery located in El Nido, Palawan, Philippines. Known for its artisan coffee, fresh juices, and globally inspired breakfast and brunch offerings, it caters to travelers exploring the island’s beach town hub. The café is celebrated for its relaxed atmosphere and emphasis on locally sourced ingredients.

Key facts
Location: 1028 Rizal Street, El Nido, Palawan Island 5313, Philippines

Cuisine: Café and brunch, with Filipino and Western influences

Specialties: Specialty coffee, smoothie bowls, and fresh pastries

Setting: Cozy, tropical-inspired interior with outdoor seating

Atmosphere and Concept
Taste El Nido blends a modern café aesthetic with a laid-back island vibe, featuring light wood décor, open-air seating, and locally made accents. It has become a popular stop for both locals and tourists seeking quality coffee and wholesome meals in a scenic coastal town known for its limestone cliffs and lagoons.

Menu and Offerings
The café’s menu focuses on fresh, flavorful dishes—smoothie bowls, avocado toasts, homemade granola, and espresso-based drinks made from locally roasted beans. Vegan and vegetarian options are common, reflecting the preferences of El Nido’s international visitor base.

Community and Appeal
Beyond food, Taste El Nido contributes to El Nido’s growing sustainable dining scene, emphasizing eco-conscious practices such as reusable straws and reduced plastic use. Its central location on Rizal Street makes it a convenient meeting spot for travelers exploring Palawan’s northern coast.
